01 — About

Design-trained. Production-tested.

I spent five years in commercial printing and campus mail services, where a misplaced decimal costs real money and deadlines don't move. So I taught myself to program — first PostScript and Ruby, then Python, SQL, and JavaScript — and started replacing the tedious parts of my own job.

That turned into ListManager, a desktop application the University of Michigan‑Flint now uses to merge, validate, and standardize mailing lists — work that helped cut costs on large-scale mailings by 75%. Since then I've built production web apps in Next.js, an automated video pipeline in Python, and machine-learning projects, and finished an M.S. in Computer Science in 2025.

The BFA in Graphic Design shows up in everything I ship: interfaces that make sense the first time you see them.
